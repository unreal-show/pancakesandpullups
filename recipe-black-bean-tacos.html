Smashed Black Bean Tacos

“Taco night, but fast. Corn tortillas, so it's gluten-free too.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • 3 corn tortillas75 g
  • ¾ cup black beans150 g
  • ⅓ avocado50 g
  • ¼ cup salsa50 g
  • shredded lettuce or cabbage30 g

Contains: none of the major allergens. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Warm beans with cumin and garlic, then smash them with a fork.
  2. Char the tortillas in a dry pan.
  3. Beans, lettuce, avocado, salsa. Lime on everything.
