Turkey Meatball Pasta

“Comfort food that still gets the job done. Make the meatballs big.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz dry whole-wheat pasta75 g
  • 4½ oz ground turkey130 g
  • ½ cup marinara125 g
  • 2 Tbsp parmesan10 g
  • 1 handful spinach30 g

Contains: gluten, dairy. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Mix turkey with garlic, Italian seasoning, salt. Roll into 6 meatballs.
  2. Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes.
  3. Cook pasta. Warm marinara with the spinach.
  4. Toss it all together. Parm on top.
