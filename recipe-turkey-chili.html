Game-Day Turkey Chili

Ingredients

  • 5 oz ground turkey150 g
  • ⅔ cup black beans120 g
  • ⅔ cup canned tomatoes150 g
  • ½ onion50 g
  • ½ bell pepper60 g
  • 2 Tbsp Greek yogurt (topping)30 g

Contains: dairy. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Brown turkey with onion and pepper.
  2. Add chili powder, cumin, garlic, oregano. Stir 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes and beans. Simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Top with Greek yogurt instead of sour cream.
