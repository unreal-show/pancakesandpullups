Chicken Burrito Bowl

“My meal-prep MVP. Cook the chicken and rice once, eat well all week.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • 5 oz chicken breast150 g
  • 1 cup cooked rice150 g
  • ½ cup black beans80 g
  • ¼ cup salsa50 g
  • ¼ avocado40 g
  • 1 handful greens40 g

Contains: none of the major allergens. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Season chicken with chili powder, cumin, garlic, salt.
  2. Pan-sear or air-fry until 165°F inside, about 6 minutes a side. Rest, then slice.
  3. Bowl: greens, rice, beans, chicken.
  4. Salsa and avocado on top. Squeeze of lime.
