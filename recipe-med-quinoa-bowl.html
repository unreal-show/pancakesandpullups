Mediterranean Chickpea Quinoa Bowl

“Holds up great in the fridge. Tastes better on day two, honestly.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup cooked quinoa150 g
  • ¾ cup chickpeas120 g
  • ⅓ cucumber60 g
  • a handful cherry tomatoes60 g
  • ¼ cup feta30 g
  • ½ Tbsp olive oil7 g

Contains: dairy. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Cook quinoa (or use leftover).
  2. Chop cucumber and tomatoes.
  3. Toss everything with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Feta on top.
