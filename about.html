About

Meet Marcus Brooks

Training every morning. Breakfast for the whole house after. This site is that routine, opened up to everyone.

Marcus Brooks is one of six AI-generated Cast Members on UNREAL, a reality series set in a glass villa in the Hollywood Hills. He's the easygoing one everyone wants to hang out with, and the one who's already finished a workout by the time the house wakes up.

His routine is simple. Train in the morning, then cook breakfast for everyone, whether they trained or not. His signature is a stack of Sweet Potato Protein Pancakes: flourless, about 50 g of protein, and the reason half the house gets up early.

Pancakes & Pull-ups is how he'd coach a friend: a plan built around your real schedule, food that's high in protein and actually good, and no guilt when life gets in the way.

How Marcus trains

  • Consistency beats intensity. Three good sessions a week for a year beats seven for a month.
  • Add a little every week. Hit the top of your rep range, then add weight. That's the whole secret.
  • Walk every day. It's the most underrated tool in fitness.
  • Sleep is training. Seven to nine hours is when you actually get better.

How Marcus eats

  • Protein first. Build every meal around it, then add carbs and color.
  • Cook once, eat twice. Most dinners make tomorrow's lunch.
  • Nothing is off-limits. Pancakes are literally in the name.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Marcus a real personal trainer or nutritionist?

No. Marcus Brooks is an AI-generated character from the reality series UNREAL. He isn't a certified personal trainer, registered dietitian, nutritionist or doctor, and nothing on this site is medical advice.

Are the plans really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email, no paywall. Build as many plans as you want.

Do you store my answers?

No. Your answers are processed in your own browser to build the plan. They aren't saved or sent anywhere.

Where do the nutrition numbers come from?

Every recipe lists ingredient weights in grams. Calories, protein, carbs and fat are calculated from USDA FoodData Central values for those weights. Brands and cooking methods vary, so treat the numbers as close estimates.

I'm a complete beginner. Will this work for me?

Yes. Pick "New" for experience and the plan keeps the moves simple, caps you at four lifting days and builds slowly. If you have an injury, a medical condition or you're pregnant, talk to your doctor first.
