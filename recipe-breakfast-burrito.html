Big Brother Breakfast Burrito

“Make five on Sunday, wrap them in foil, freeze. Microwave 2 minutes.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • 1 large whole-wheat tortilla70 g
  • 2 eggs100 g
  • ⅓ cup egg whites100 g
  • ⅓ cup black beans60 g
  • 1 handful spinach30 g
  • 2 Tbsp shredded mozzarella20 g
  • 2 Tbsp salsa40 g

Contains: gluten, eggs, dairy. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Scramble eggs and egg whites in a nonstick pan. Toss in spinach at the end.
  2. Warm the beans and the tortilla.
  3. Fill: eggs, beans, cheese, salsa. Roll it tight, tuck the ends.
  4. Toast seam-side down 1 minute so it holds together.
