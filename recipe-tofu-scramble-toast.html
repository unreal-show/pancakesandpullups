Tofu Scramble on Toast

“All the comfort of scrambled eggs, fully plant-based.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • ½ block firm tofu150 g
  • 1 big handful spinach40 g
  • ⅓ bell pepper, diced50 g
  • ¼ onion, diced30 g
  • 1 tsp olive oil5 g
  • 2 slices whole-wheat toast70 g

Contains: soy, gluten. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Oil in a pan on medium. Cook onion and pepper 3 minutes.
  2. Crumble the tofu in with your hands. Add a pinch of turmeric, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Cook 5 minutes, stirring. Fold in spinach until it wilts.
  4. Pile it on toast.
