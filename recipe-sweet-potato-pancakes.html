Sweet Potato Protein Pancakes

“My signature stack. Flourless, high protein, and it tastes like a weekend. Make a double batch and freeze the extras.” — Marcus

Ingredients

  • ½ cup mashed cooked sweet potato (about 1 small)120 g
  • ½ cup rolled oats40 g
  • 2 eggs100 g
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey30 g
  • ⅓ cup Greek yogurt (topping)75 g
  • a handful of berries60 g
  • 2 tsp maple syrup10 g

Contains: gluten, eggs, dairy. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Cook the sweet potato: prick it and microwave 5–6 minutes until soft, or use leftover roasted. Scoop out ½ cup and let it cool a little.
  2. Blend the sweet potato, oats, eggs and whey with 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cinnamon, a pinch of nutmeg and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  3. Let the batter rest 5 minutes. It thickens as the oats soak.
  4. Heat a nonstick pan on medium-low with a light coat of oil. Pour 3-inch pancakes.
  5. Cook 2–3 minutes until the edges set, flip, then 1–2 minutes more. Keep the heat lower than usual: sweet potato browns fast.
  6. Stack them up. Top with Greek yogurt, berries and maple syrup.
