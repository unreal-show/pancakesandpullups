Shrimp Fajita Bowl

Ingredients

  • 5 oz shrimp150 g
  • ¾ cup cooked brown rice150 g
  • 1 bell pepper100 g
  • ½ onion50 g
  • ½ Tbsp olive oil7 g
  • 2 Tbsp salsa30 g

Contains: shellfish. Always check your labels. Salt, pepper and spices not listed.

Steps

  1. Slice peppers and onion. Cook in the oil on high, 5 minutes.
  2. Season shrimp with fajita spice. Add to the pan, 2 minutes a side.
  3. Rice in a bowl, everything on top, salsa and lime.
